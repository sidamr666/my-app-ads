Community Guidelines

Effective 26 September 2026

Chess Meet is for chess — online, and across a real board. These rules keep it that way. They apply everywhere in the app: games, chats, profiles, meetups, places and tournaments.

Meeting in person (Meet & Play, 18+)

  • Public places only — the listed café, club or venue. Never a home, a car, or “somewhere quieter”.
  • Never share a live location, an exact position or your address, and never ask for someone else’s.
  • Keep personal details private: phone number, school, workplace.
  • Tell someone you trust where you are going and when. The app’s “Share my plan” does this in one tap.
  • Keep to the plan. Pressure to change the place or meet somewhere private is a reason to leave, block and report.
  • Report results honestly. Both players confirm a game with a code exchanged at the board; results only count when you both agree.

Not allowed anywhere

  • Harassment: contact that continues after someone says no, insults, stalking.
  • Threats of harm, in any words.
  • Sexual messages, images or advances. Anything sexual involving a minor is removed, banned and reported to the authorities — see child safety.
  • Hate: attacks on people for who they are.
  • Scams: asking for money, selling, suspicious links.
  • Pretending to be someone else, or using a false age.
  • Cheating: engine help during a game, playing someone else’s games, deliberately losing, or using several accounts — including to enter the same tournament twice.
  • Fake places or fake tournaments, and organising a tournament you do not intend to run.

Tournaments

  • Organisers run the event they listed, at the place and time they listed.
  • If you register for an in-person tournament, turn up or withdraw in time. Missing two you registered for within 60 days pauses registration for 30 days.

Reporting and blocking

Report from a player’s profile, a meetup chat message, a meetup, a tournament or a place. Block from a profile: it stops all contact both ways at once, and the other player is not told. Every report is read by a person.

What happens when rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is: a warning, removal of the content, a suspension from Meet & Play or from the app, or a permanent ban. Anything that puts someone in danger is acted on at once, and may be passed to the police.
