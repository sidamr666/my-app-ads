Privacy Policy

Effective 26 September 2026

This policy explains what the Chess Meet app (also shown as “Let’s Chess”) collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how to delete it. It covers the Android app and this website.

Who we are. Chess Meet (“we”), the developer of the Chess Meet app on Google Play. Contact: chessmeet.contact@gmail.com.
The short version.
  • You need an account to play. We store your profile, games and ratings.
  • Meet & Play (for adults only) uses your location to find players and places near you. Other players never see your location — only a distance band such as “2–5 km”. Your last position is deleted after 30 days without use.
  • We show ads through Google AdMob. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you choose whether ads are personalised.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time.

1. What we collect

Your account

  • Email address, and a password handled by Firebase Authentication (we never see it). If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account’s email and name.
  • Username, profile picture (if you add one), status message, and the country or region you choose for leaderboards.

Playing

  • Your games: moves, results, time controls, opponents. Ratings and rating history. Puzzle and opening-training progress.
  • Online games are relayed live through our servers while they are played.
  • When you ask for a game review, the positions (not your identity) are sent to our analysis server.
  • Tournament registrations and results, for online and in-person tournaments.

Friends and messages

  • Friend requests, your friends list, game invitations, and chat messages with friends.
  • Whether you are online or in a game, shown to other players.
  • Players you block, and reports you file (including, when you report a message, the text of that message).

Meet & Play — only if you use it (18+)

  • Date of birth. Asked once when you first open Meet & Play, to keep it adults-only. It is private: no other player can see it, and it cannot be changed afterwards.
  • Precise location. While you use Meet & Play screens, your phone sends its position to our server, which compares it with other players and returns only distance bands (1–2, 2–5, 5–10, 10–20 or 20–50 km). Your exact position is stored on our server as your last known position and is never sent to other players. It is also used to tell you about new in-person tournaments within 50 km, and to check the 50 km rule when you join one. It is deleted automatically after 30 days without a refresh, and immediately when you delete your account. The app does not use location in the background.
  • “Available to play” status, shown to other players while it is on; only adults near you can find you through it.
  • Meet requests and meetups: the public place and time you choose, the meetup chat (which refuses locations, map links and coordinates, and closes 24 hours after the meeting), the confirmation code you exchange in person, and the result you both report. Confirmed results update an over-the-board rating shown on your profile.
  • Places: “I’m here now” check-ins at a venue (removed when you leave or disconnect) and “I’m coming tonight” — other players see only how many, never who. Places you suggest, with the details you enter.

Device and app

  • A push-notification token for each device you sign in on.
  • A random install ID, kept on your phone and used only to stop two accounts from the same phone entering the same online tournament room.
  • Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are used only for two-phone local play; that game data goes directly between the two phones.
  • Basic technical logs kept by our hosting provider (for example, errors and request times).
  • An integrity check (Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity) confirming that requests come from the genuine app on a real device. It uses signals about the device and the app install, not your messages or games.

Ads

Google AdMob shows ads in the app and uses your device’s advertising ID. Ads are limited to content rated for teenagers. If we know you are under 16 (from the Meet & Play date of birth), your ad requests are marked as under the age of consent. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google’s consent message, and can change it any time in Settings → Privacy settings.

3. Who can see what

  • Other players see your public profile: username, picture, country, ratings, game counts, trophies and trust level, and whether you are online. Nearby adults using Meet & Play see a distance band — never your position.
  • Moderators can read reports and the data needed to act on them.
  • Service providers process data for us under their terms: Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging, Cloud Run, AdMob and Google Sign-In) and the map-tile provider that draws the map (it receives your IP address and the map area you view).
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety — including reports of child sexual abuse material to the competent authorities.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it for anyone else’s marketing.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account, games and friends: until you delete your account.
  • Meet & Play position: 30 days after its last refresh, or until you delete your account.
  • Live game rooms: about 7 days after the game ends; your game history stays in your account.
  • Reports and moderation records: kept after account deletion for safety and legal reasons.
  • Meetups and tournament results: kept, because they are also other players’ history.

5. Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Delete account. This deletes your sign-in, profile, rating history, progress, friends and friend chats, game history, date of birth, location, block list, username and profile picture. Details and the no-app route are on Delete your account.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent at any time (turn off location, “Available”, or change the ad choice in Settings). Write to chessmeet.contact@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. In the EEA or UK you can also complain to your data-protection authority.

7. Children

Chess Meet is for people aged 13 and over. Meet & Play is for adults (18+) only and is locked for younger accounts. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, we delete it. See our child safety standards.

8. Security and transfers

Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) and stored with Google Cloud, which may process it outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union, under Google’s standard contractual clauses. Access rules on our databases stop players from reading each other’s private data. Your exact location is readable only by our servers, and your date of birth only by you and our servers.

9. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change applies. The date at the top always shows the current version.
