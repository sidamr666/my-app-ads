Child Safety Standards

Chess Meet (Let’s Chess) · Effective 26 September 2026

Zero tolerance. Chess Meet prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) of any kind, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, and any sexualised contact with, or content involving, a minor. Accounts that do this are removed permanently and reported to the authorities.

Who the app is for

  • Chess Meet is for people aged 13 and over.
  • Meet & Play — finding players nearby, meeting in person, local chess places and in-person tournaments — is for adults (18+) only. Every account gives a date of birth once, through a neutral form that does not say which age is needed; it cannot be changed afterwards. Younger accounts cannot open Meet & Play, and our servers refuse its features to them even if the app is modified.

How the app is built to prevent harm

  • No locations between players. Other players see only a distance band such as “2–5 km”, never a position.
  • Public places only. Meetups can only be set at listed public places (cafés, clubs, federations, public venues), never a private address.
  • No location sharing in chat. Meetup chats refuse map links, coordinates and location codes, open only after a request is accepted, and close 24 hours after the meeting.
  • Blocking stops all contact both ways at once: nearby discovery, requests, invitations and messages.
  • No images in chat. Chats are text only.
  • Safety guidance is shown before anyone first uses Meet & Play.

How to report

In the app

  • On any player’s profile: Report.
  • On a meetup chat message: press and hold it, then Report.
  • On a meetup, a tournament or a place: the Report option in its menu.

Reports reach our moderators by email as soon as they are filed, and are reviewed as a priority when they involve a minor.

By email

chessmeet.contact@gmail.com — include the username and what happened. Do not send us images of abuse; describe it and we will act on the account.

If a child is in immediate danger

Contact your local police or emergency number first. You can also report to NCMEC’s CyberTipline (United States), the Internet Watch Foundation (UK), or the hotline in your country listed by INHOPE.

What we do

  • Suspend the account at once while we review, and remove content.
  • Ban accounts that commit CSAE, permanently.
  • Report CSAM and CSAE to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the competent authorities, and preserve what the law requires.
  • Cooperate with law-enforcement requests made through proper legal process.

Contact for child safety

Our designated contact for child-safety matters, including for Google Play and authorities: chessmeet.contact@gmail.com.
