Delete your Chess Meet account

Chess Meet (Let’s Chess) · Effective 26 September 2026

In the app — immediate

  1. Open Chess Meet and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If the app asks you to sign in again first, that is a security check by the sign-in service; sign in and repeat step 3.

Without the app

Email chessmeet.contact@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my account” and your username. We confirm and delete within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in and your profile (username, picture, status, country).
  • Ratings and rating history, puzzle and opening progress.
  • Your game history.
  • Friend requests, friendships and the chats in them.
  • Meet & Play: your date of birth, your last known position, your block list and any place suggestion still under review.
  • Push-notification tokens and live status (online, in game, tournament waiting rooms).
  • Your reserved username, so it becomes free again.

What is kept, and why

  • Reports and moderation records — safety records. Deleting an account must not erase a report made about it. Kept only as long as safety or the law requires.
  • Meetups and tournament results you took part in — they are also the other players’ history. Your name there is the username you had.
  • Online game rooms — removed automatically about 7 days after each game.

Deletion cannot be undone. A new account starts from nothing.
